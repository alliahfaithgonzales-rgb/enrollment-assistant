# Sprint Plan

## User Stories

### 1. Incoming Freshman
*As* an incoming freshman, *I want* quick answers about enrollment requirements and deadlines, *so that* I don't miss important dates.
- ✅ Acceptance Criteria: Shows clear requirements list; Displays exact enrollment dates; Works in English/Taglish

### 2. Parent
*As* a parent, *I want* clear tuition payment options and schedules, *so that* I can plan our budget accordingly.
- ✅ Acceptance Criteria: Lists payment plans; Shows deadlines for each installment; No hidden fees mentioned

### 3. Registrar Staff
*As* registrar staff, *I want* common questions answered automatically, *so that* I don't have to repeat the same replies every day.
- ✅ Acceptance Criteria: Only answers from approved FAQ; Admits when it doesn't know; Shows which FAQ entries were used
